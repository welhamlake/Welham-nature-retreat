Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is the wood-fired hot tub included?

Yes, private use of the wood-fired hot tub is included in the luxury cabin's £300 nightly rate.

Does accommodation include the wildlife hide?

No. The wildlife photography hide is independently bookable, subject to availability, at £75 for one person or £100 for two.

When can I check in?

Accommodation check-in is from 3pm, and check-out is by 10:30am. Arrival days are Monday through Friday.

Can I book the island bell tent now?

Not yet. The island experience is under construction, with a planned opening in March 2027. We will announce when reservations are available.

When do I pay?

After your request is approved, we will send a Stripe payment link for the 50% deposit. The remaining 50% is payable on arrival by cash, card or bank transfer.
