Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is the wood-fired hot tub included?

Yes, private use of the wood-fired hot tub is included in the luxury cabin's £300 nightly rate.

Does accommodation include wildlife hides?

Yes. The luxury cabin includes its dedicated wildlife hide throughout the stay, including checkout day. Island Glamping includes exclusive use of all three island wildlife hides throughout the stay, including checkout day. The Woodland Wildlife Pool Hide can be booked independently.

Can I book several days in the Woodland Wildlife Pool Hide?

Yes. You can request 1–7 consecutive daytime sessions (7am–5pm) or overnight sessions (6pm–6am the following morning). Each session costs £75 for one person or £100 for two people. All selected sessions must be available.

Can I book the cabin wildlife hide without staying in the cabin?

Yes. The Luxury Cabin Wildlife Hide is available for exclusive daytime photography sessions from 7am to 5pm, for £75 for one person or £100 for two. Requests can be made up to 14 days ahead, provided no cabin stay or other booking reserves the hide. The cabin's checkout day is also unavailable for hide-only bookings.

When can I check in?

Accommodation check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 10:30am. Cabin arrivals are Monday through Friday.

When can I book the island?

Requests for Island Glamping stays from 1 March 2027 can be submitted, subject to the facilities being completed. Island Wildlife Hide sessions become bookable 14 days before each session, starting with the March 2027 opening.

Can I book the island hides while someone is glamping?

No. Island Glamping guests have exclusive use of the three island hides, including their checkout day.

When do I pay?

After approval, we email bank transfer instructions for full payment, due within 24 hours. Your booking is confirmed after we verify payment.
